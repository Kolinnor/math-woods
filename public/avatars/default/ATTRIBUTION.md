[Animal Outlined Sepia Icons](https://www.svgrepo.com/collection/animal-outlined-sepia-icons/)
collection by AsIan, distributed via [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com/).

They are used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The SVG artwork is used unchanged; Math Woods supplies the surrounding background color.
